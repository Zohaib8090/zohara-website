05/Link
01Zohara Link In development

Your phone and your computer, together.

A companion for Android that shares your clipboard and files, shows your phone’s notifications on the desktop, and turns the phone into a touchpad, a keyboard and a game controller.

Not released. The computer side is in the development channel. The Android app is a test build that is not on any store: it installs from GitHub and has had builds and tests, but little use on real phones yet.

02Share

Clipboard, files, notifications.

A

Clipboard

Copy on one, paste on the other.

B

Files

Send a file from the phone to the computer, or from the computer to the phone.

C

Notifications and media

Phone notifications appear on the desktop; play, pause and skip from the phone.

03Control

A touchpad, a keyboard and a game controller.

The computer makes virtual input devices and writes the phone’s events straight to them, so the pointer follows your finger instead of lagging behind it.

  • Touchpad. Pointer acceleration, tap to click, two-finger tap to right-click, two-finger drag to scroll smoothly, tap-then-drag to hold the button.
  • Keyboard keys, including held keys, and typing.
  • Game controller. The phone appears as a wired Xbox 360 controller, the one every game and Steam already know: two sticks, two triggers, a direction pad and eleven buttons.
  • Full-screen remote. See the screen and control it as a touchpad or a touch screen.
You decide. A phone may only see the screen or control the mouse when you switch that on for it in Settings > Zohara Link. Both are off until you choose.
04Game streaming

Live video from the computer to the phone. In development

The screen is captured through the desktop’s own screen-sharing permission, encoded with a hardware video encoder, and sent as live video with WebRTC. The phone decodes it with its own hardware.

  • Hardware encoding. The Intel chip’s encoder first, so the NVIDIA chip can stay asleep; NVENC when the NVIDIA chip is already running the game; software as a last resort.
  • Encrypted end to end by WebRTC, set up over the connection the phone is already paired on.
  • Input travels back on the same stream.
  • One-time permission. The desktop asks once whether Zohara Link may share the screen.
Honest status. The pieces are built and tested separately: a whole test stream between two programs, the signalling against the real daemon, and the screen-sharing permission on a real laptop. A stream to a real phone has not been tried yet, and delay has not been measured. Sound is not built.
05Private by design

Nothing leaves your network.

A

A PIN you type once

Pairing is closed until you press Pair a phone. The PIN is shown on the computer and typed on the phone; it never travels over the network.

B

Pinned and encrypted

The connection is TLS with the computer’s certificate pinned on the phone.

C

Away from home

With Tailscale on both, the phone reaches the computer from any network. Zohara Link can sign in and out of Tailscale for you.
